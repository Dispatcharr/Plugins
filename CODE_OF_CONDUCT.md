# Code of Conduct

Dispatcharr welcomes contributions that help people manage content they are authorized to access and distribute. Contributors are responsible for ensuring that their plugins, documentation, and support activities comply with applicable law and the rights of content owners.

## Repository rules

Contributions must not:

1. Include, distribute, link to, or facilitate access to illegal content.
2. Enable copyright infringement, unauthorized access, credential theft, or evasion of access controls.
3. Are designed to facilitate reselling IPTV services, access to content, or third-party services without authorization from the relevant rights holder.
4. Require payment, a subscription, or a paid service operated by the plugin's publisher as a condition of using the plugin. Plugins may integrate with external paid services that users independently choose to obtain and pay for.
5. Misrepresent the origin, availability, pricing, or licensing of content, services, or software.
6. Collect, expose, or misuse personal information, credentials, API keys, or other confidential data.
7. Contact an external service for telemetry, analytics, licensing, updates, or other phone-home behavior without clearly labeling that behavior and obtaining the user's explicit consent.
8. Are abandonware, including submissions without a committed maintainer or a plan for maintenance, transfer, or removal. (These may be removed or unlisted without warning at repository maintainer discretion.)
9. Violate the license, terms of service, or acceptable-use policy of a service that the plugin integrates with.
10. Submit a pull request with a blank or materially incomplete description. Doing so may result in auto-merge privileges being revoked.

These rules may change at any time without notice, at the discretion of the Dispatcharr team.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected violations privately to a project maintainer through the [Dispatcharr Discord](https://discord.gg/Sp45V5BcxU). Include links or other evidence when possible. Maintainers will review reports promptly and handle them with discretion.

This Code of Conduct guides maintainer decisions but does not limit their authority over repository submissions. Maintainers may remove, deny, or restrict submissions; close issues or pull requests; disable distribution; or ban contributors.
