# Code of Conduct

Dispatcharr welcomes contributions that help people manage content they are authorized to access and distribute. Contributors are responsible for ensuring that their plugins, documentation, and support activities comply with applicable law and the rights of content owners.

## Repository rules

1. **No illegal or unauthorized activity:** Do not include, distribute, link to, facilitate access to, or enable illegal content, copyright infringement, unauthorized access, credential theft, or evasion of access controls.
2. **No unauthorized resale:** Do not submit contributions designed to facilitate reselling IPTV services, access to content, or third-party services without authorization from the relevant rights holder.
3. **No paid plugins:** Do not require payment, a subscription, or a paid service operated by the plugin's publisher as a condition of using the plugin. Plugins may integrate with external paid services that users independently choose to obtain and pay for.
4. **No undisclosed phone-home behavior:** Do not contact an external service for telemetry, analytics, licensing, updates, or other phone-home behavior without clearly labeling that behavior and obtaining the user's explicit consent.
5. **No abandonware:** Do not submit abandonware or submissions without a committed maintainer or a plan for maintenance, transfer, or removal. (These may be removed or unlisted without warning at repository maintainer discretion.)
6. **No third-party terms violations:** Do not violate the license, terms of service, or acceptable-use policy of a service that the plugin integrates with.
7. **No sandbox or capability-list circumvention:** Do not bypass the plugin sandbox or circumvent required capability declarations (for manifest versions where they are required).
8. **No incomplete pull requests:** Do not submit a pull request with a blank or materially incomplete description. Doing so may result in auto-merge privileges being revoked.
9. **Open source licensing required:** Every plugin must be released under an OSI-approved open source license.
10. **Automated submissions must be deliberate and compliant:** Automation is acceptable when it follows every other repository rule, including ownership, licensing, security, validation, rate limits, and meaningful pull request descriptions. Do not use automation that opens or updates a registry pull request for every source push, commit, or branch update. Stable and prerelease submissions must be started deliberately by an author or maintainer, or by a workflow triggered from a deliberate release event or release-oriented workflow action.

These rules may change at any time without notice, at the discretion of the Dispatcharr team.

## Disclaimer

- All external tools, integrations, and services are **used at your own risk**.
- Users are responsible for verifying the integrity and behavior of any code they choose to run.
- The Dispatcharr team is **not liable** for any damage, data loss, or security issues resulting from the use of shared tools or services.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected violations privately to a project maintainer through the [Dispatcharr Discord](https://discord.gg/Sp45V5BcxU). Include links or other evidence when possible. Maintainers will review reports promptly and handle them with discretion.

This Code of Conduct guides maintainer decisions but does not limit their authority over repository submissions. Maintainers may remove, deny, or restrict submissions; close issues or pull requests; disable distribution; or ban contributors.
