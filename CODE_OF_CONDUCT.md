# Code of Conduct

Dispatcharr welcomes contributions that help people manage content they are authorized to access and distribute. Contributors are responsible for ensuring that their plugins, documentation, and support activities comply with applicable law and the rights of content owners.

## Contribution boundaries

Do not submit or promote contributions that:

- Include, distribute, link to, or facilitate access to illegal content.
- Enable copyright infringement, unauthorized access, credential theft, or evasion of access controls.
- Are designed to facilitate reselling IPTV services, access to content, or third-party services without authorization from the relevant rights holder.
- Require payment, a subscription, or a paid service operated by the plugin's publisher as a condition of using the plugin. Plugins may integrate with external paid services that users independently choose to obtain and pay for.
- Misrepresent the origin, availability, pricing, or licensing of content, services, or software.
- Collect, expose, or misuse personal information, credentials, API keys, or other confidential data.
- Contact an external service for telemetry, analytics, licensing, updates, or other phone-home behavior without clearly labeling that behavior and obtaining the user's explicit consent.
- Are abandonware, including submissions without a committed maintainer or a plan for maintenance, transfer, or removal. (These may be removed or unlisted without warning at repository maintainer discretion.)
- Violate the license, terms of service, or acceptable-use policy of a service that the plugin integrates with.

These rules apply to plugin source, release artifacts, metadata, documentation, pull requests, issues, discussions, and linked materials.

## Expected conduct

Treat other community members with respect. Keep discussion focused on the project, give constructive feedback, and do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish another person's private information without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected violations privately to a project maintainer through the [Dispatcharr Discord](https://discord.gg/Sp45V5BcxU). Include links or other evidence when possible. Maintainers will review reports promptly and handle them with discretion.

This Code of Conduct guides maintainer decisions but does not limit their authority over repository submissions. Maintainers may remove, deny, or restrict submissions; close issues or pull requests; disable distribution; or ban contributors.
